How it works

A bounded month. One approval. You stay in control.

You give us one call and the photos you already take. You get back a month of posts, checked and ready to paste.

The month, step by step.

  1. Intake call

    You tell us about your services, clients and voice.

    We write it down as your brand file: approved words, banned words, hard rules.

  2. Photos in

    You share photos from your projects: phone shots, site visits, stories, finished rooms. Messy is fine.

    Add a short voice note per project. We turn it into a facts sheet.

  3. The month is built

    We enhance your photos with AI and write posts for your platforms only.

    Light, colour, cleanup and angles. The room stays exactly as built. Every fact comes from you.

  4. Seven-point review

    Banned words, facts, your rules, format, voice, theme and every photo edit.

    A piece that fails is fixed or held back.

  5. You approve

    One session a month, about 20 minutes.

    You see every edited photo beside its original. Nothing ships without your yes.

  6. You publish

    You post from your own account, with a ready-to-paste pack and a checklist.

    We report what shipped, from the numbers you share.

Your part, in full.

  • One intake call at the start.
  • Photos from your sites, any quality.
  • A short voice note per project.
  • One approval session a month.

Lines we hold.

  • We never log in to your accounts or post for you.
  • We never use AI to add, remove or change anything in your design.
  • We never show a stock room as your work.
  • We never invent a number, an award or a client quote.
  • We never message your clients.
