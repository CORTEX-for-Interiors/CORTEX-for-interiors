How it works

A planned month. Weekly drops. You stay in control.

You give us one call and the photos you already take. You get a content strategy and posts every week.

The month, step by step.

  1. Intake call

    You tell us about your services, clients and voice.

    We write it down as your brand file: approved words, banned words, hard rules.

  2. Photos in

    Share project photos in a folder on your own Drive. Messy is fine.

    Send a short voice note per project on WhatsApp. We turn it into a facts sheet.

  3. The plan

    A 20-minute call each month sets what to post and when.

    You approve the plan. Nothing is written before that.

  4. Weekly drops

    Every week we enhance your photos and write posts for your platforms.

    Light, colour, cleanup and angles. The room stays exactly as built. Every fact comes from you. Plus a weekly Story kit: about five frames from that week's site photos.

  5. Seven-point review

    Banned words, facts, your rules, format, voice, theme and every photo edit.

    A piece that fails is fixed or held back.

  6. Your OK

    A quick yes on WhatsApp for each drop.

    You see every edited photo beside its original. Two rewrite rounds on every post.

  7. You publish

    Post from your own account with a ready-to-paste pack.

    On Studio and Signature, we can post what you approved and answer public comments for you, through a role you grant. We report back from the numbers you share.

Your part, in full.

  • One intake call at the start.
  • Photos from your sites, any quality, in a shared folder.
  • A short voice note per project.
  • A 20-minute plan call each month.
  • A quick OK on each weekly drop.

Lines we hold.

  • We never ask for your password.
  • We post for you only if you choose it, through a role you grant.
  • We never use AI to add, remove or change anything in your design.
  • We never show a stock room as your work.
  • We never invent a number, an award or a client quote.
  • We never send your clients direct messages. Their messages stay with you.
